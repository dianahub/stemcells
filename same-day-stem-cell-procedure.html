The same-day stem cell procedure, step by step

Harvesting your own fat, preparing it on site, and injecting it all happen in a single clinic visit — with local anesthesia only, no operating room, and no general anesthesia. Here is what each step involves and how long it takes.

What “same-day” actually means

The whole process is completed in one outpatient visit. You are awake the entire time, only local anesthetic is used at the harvest and injection sites, and there is no operating room and no general anesthesia. Most people arrive, complete the procedure, spend a short observation period, and go home the same day.

Because the cells come from your own fat (an autologous source), there is no donor matching and nothing is cultured or sent to an outside lab — the tissue is prepared in the clinic and used in the same session.

  • One visit

    Harvest, preparation, and injection happen back-to-back in the same appointment.

  • Local anesthesia only

  • Your own tissue

  • Home the same day

The steps

A typical visit moves through six stages. Timing varies by person and by how many areas are being treated.

1

Consultation & assessment

It starts with a free video consultation. Amedsi's staff review your history and goals and decide whether the procedure is appropriate for you. Candidacy is always their clinical call.

2

Fat harvest

On the day, a small area (often the lower abdomen or flank) is numbed with local anesthetic and a modest amount of fat is collected through a small minimally invasive draw.

3

On-site preparation

The fat is processed in the clinic to isolate the cell-rich fraction. This happens while you wait — nothing is shipped out or grown over days.

4

Injection or application

The prepared material is injected into the target joint (for example a knee, shoulder, hip, or the lower back) or applied to the scalp for hair thinning, again under local anesthetic.

5

Recovery & aftercare

A short observation period, then you go home the same day. You are given guidance on activity, soreness at the harvest and injection sites, and what to watch for.

6

Follow-up

Clinic staff follow up afterward. Some patients report changes over a period of weeks to months; individual results vary, are assessed case by case, and are not guaranteed.

How long it takes

Plan for most of a half-day at the clinic. The consultation happens beforehand by video, so the in-person visit is the harvest, the preparation wait, the injection, and a short recovery window. Treating more than one joint adds time to the injection step.

Soreness at the harvest site and the treated area for a few days is common. Your care team will tell you when to ease back into normal activity — there is no lengthy hospital stay because there is no surgery in an operating room.

Exact timings, what to bring, and pre-visit instructions come from Amedsi's staff during your consultation. Follow their guidance over anything on this page.

How it differs from PRP

PRP (platelet-rich plasma) concentrates platelets and growth factors separated from a sample of your blood by spinning it. This procedure instead isolates the cell-rich fraction from your own fat. Both are outpatient, completed the same day, and use only your own tissue — they differ in the source and in what is concentrated.

What people ask about it for

The two most common reasons are ongoing joint discomfort — knees, shoulders, hips, and the lower back — and thinning hair. The procedure is the same in structure for both; the difference is where the prepared material goes.

Common questions

Is it really done in a single day?

Yes. The fat is harvested, prepared on site, and injected during one clinic visit. The consultation is done separately by video beforehand.

Will I be put under?

No. Local anesthesia only. There is no general anesthesia and no operating room; you stay awake and the visit is outpatient.

Where is the fat taken from?

Usually a small area of the lower abdomen or flank, numbed with local anesthetic and collected through a small minimally invasive draw.

How soon might I notice anything?

According to Amedsi Stem Cells Clinics, some patients report changes over weeks to months. Individual results vary, are assessed case by case, and are not guaranteed.

Is this the same as PRP?

No. PRP concentrates platelets from your blood; this isolates the cell-rich fraction from your fat. Both are same-day and use your own tissue.
