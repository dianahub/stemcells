How the same-day stem cell procedure works

The home page covers this in brief. Here is the fuller version: what happens at each stage of the visit — harvesting your own fat, preparing it on site, and injecting it — and what recovery tends to look like afterward.

1. Fat harvesting

The procedure starts by collecting a small amount of your own fat through a minimally invasive draw, usually from the lower abdomen or flank. This step uses local anesthesia only — there is no general anesthesia and no operating room, and you stay awake throughout.

According to Amedsi: the amount collected is modest — on the order of about 100 cc — and the draw itself takes only a portion of the visit. Individual experience varies, and the exact approach is decided by the clinical team for each person.

2. On-site preparation

During the same visit, the fat sample is processed inside the clinic to concentrate the cell-rich portion used for treatment. Nothing is sent to an outside lab, cultured over days, or frozen for a later appointment — preparation and treatment happen in one sitting.

3. Targeted injection, same visit

Once prepared, the cell-rich material is injected the same day into the area being treated — for example a knee, shoulder, hip, or the lower back, or into the scalp for hair thinning. Placement is done by the clinical team based on your assessment, again under local anesthetic.

Treating more than one area in a single visit is possible and lengthens this step.

4. Recovery and downtime

Because there is no surgical incision in an operating room, there is no hospital stay. After a short observation period you go home the same day with aftercare guidance covering activity, soreness at the harvest and injection sites, and what to watch for.

According to Amedsi: some patients describe changes in comfort or mobility over a period of weeks to months, tracked by clinical staff at follow-up visits. Individual results vary, are assessed case by case, and are not guaranteed.

Exact pre-visit instructions, what to bring, and timing come from Amedsi's staff during your consultation. Follow their guidance over anything on this page.

Who typically asks about this

The two most common reasons people ask are ongoing joint discomfort — knees, shoulders, hips, and the lower back — and thinning hair. The structure of the visit is the same in both cases; what differs is where the prepared material is placed.

This is general framing, not a diagnosis and not a statement that any given person is a candidate. Whether the procedure is appropriate for you is decided by Amedsi's clinical staff after a consultation.

Common questions

Is it really done in one day?

Yes. The fat is harvested, prepared on site, and injected during a single clinic visit. The consultation is done separately beforehand.

Will I be put under?

No. Local anesthesia only, at the harvest and injection sites. There is no general anesthesia and no operating room.

Is the fat sample sent to a lab?

No. According to Amedsi, the sample is processed within the clinic during the same visit — not shipped out or grown over days.

How soon might I notice a difference?

According to Amedsi, some patients report changes over weeks to months. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed.
