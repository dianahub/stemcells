How the same-day procedure works

Three steps, completed in one appointment. You stay awake throughout and go home the same day.

01

Fat harvesting

A small amount of fat is collected from your own body through a minimally invasive draw. This step uses local anesthesia only — there is no general anesthesia and no operating room.

02

On-site preparation

During the same visit, the sample is processed in the clinic to concentrate the cell-rich portion used for treatment. Nothing is shipped elsewhere or frozen for another day.

03

Targeted injection

The prepared material is injected into the treatment area the same day, placed by the clinical team based on your assessment.

Joint and pain-related questions

One reason people ask about this procedure is ongoing joint discomfort — knees, shoulders, hips, and the lower back are common examples. The approach discussed with patients is to place cell-rich material from your own body near the area that hurts, during a single outpatient visit.

According to Amedsi: some patients describe changes in comfort and mobility over a period of weeks after treatment, with clinical staff tracking progress at follow-up visits. Individual results vary and are assessed case by case.

This is general information, not a promise of any particular result. Whether the procedure is appropriate for you is decided by Amedsi's clinical staff.

Hair thinning questions

The same approach using your own tissue is also discussed for thinning hair and areas of reduced density. The concept described to patients is to introduce cell-rich material from your own body into the scalp during one clinic visit, again under local anesthesia only.

According to Amedsi: some patients report gradual changes in hair density over a course of months, evaluated by the clinical team at follow-up visits. Results differ from person to person and are not guaranteed.

Nothing here should be read as a guaranteed outcome. Suitability and realistic expectations are set by Amedsi's clinicians, not by this referral page.

Stem cell procedure vs. PRP

Both approaches use material taken from your own body and returned the same day. They differ in what is collected and what is concentrated. This overview is educational and simplified.

Aspect Autologous stem cell procedure Platelet-rich plasma (PRP)
Where the material comes from Your own fat tissue, collected through a small draw. Your own blood, collected through a standard draw.
What is concentrated The cell-rich fraction separated from the fat sample. Platelets and growth factors separated from the blood by spinning.
Typical setting Outpatient clinic visit, local anesthesia only. Outpatient clinic visit, usually topical or local numbing.
Same-day Yes — harvest, prepare, and inject in one visit. Yes — draw, spin, and inject in one visit.
Commonly asked about for Joint discomfort and hair thinning, among others. Joint discomfort, skin, and hair thinning, among others.

This table is a general comparison for orientation only. Amedsi's clinical staff determine which option, if any, is appropriate for a given person.
